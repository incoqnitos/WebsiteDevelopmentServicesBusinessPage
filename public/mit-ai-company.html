Mobile Intelligence Technologies 1985 Ltd (MIT AI 1985) is an AI and software company headquartered in Mannheim, Germany. Founded by Dimitar Konstantinov Totev, the company develops large language models, custom AI solutions, SaaS platforms, mobile and web applications, blockchain systems, digital health tools and business automation for clients across Germany and Europe.
