Research Paper Proposed Framework — Requires Independent Peer Review

TOZ-Modified Navier–Stokes Equations: Adaptive Regularization Framework

A theoretical framework investigating adaptive regularization of the Navier–Stokes equations through state-dependent effective viscosity governed by TOZ/TROK coefficients. Formal energy estimates are derived under four structural assumptions: uniform ellipticity, Lipschitz continuity, adaptive gradient damping, and Lyapunov control. The work explores whether adaptive dissipation may prevent finite-time blow-up. Results are a proposed framework requiring further mathematical verification and independent peer review.
